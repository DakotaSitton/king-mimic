> Confidence tags:
> - **[OWNER-CANONICAL]** — name comes from owner's OneDrive design files or from MECHANICS.md (which was assembled from content.js, itself transcribed from the owner's paper set)

> - **[MISSING]** — a slot exists but no surviving owner name was found
